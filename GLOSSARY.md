# Mercatorio Prestige Farming

This context compares the market cost of products and activities against their prestige value to support efficient prestige farming.

## Language

**Church contract**:
A user-added church contribution described by a product, amount, and prestige value. Its market cost can be compared with other prestige opportunities.

**Recipe profit ranking**:
A ranking of recipes by estimated profit at a town's market. Profit accounts for scaled input costs, the base operating cost, and proceeds from outputs that can be sold.
